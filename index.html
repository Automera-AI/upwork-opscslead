What a good client update covers

01   Where the work stands

Progress against a defined milestone, without vague assurances.

02   What is blocking delivery

Name the dependency, the mitigation and its owner.

03   What the client needs to decide

Make the request specific, actionable and time-bound.

04   When they will hear from us

Commit to the next update even if there is no resolution yet.
